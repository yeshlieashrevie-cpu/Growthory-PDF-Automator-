    1. Go to **github.com** and sign up for a free account.
    2. Once logged in, look for a green button that says **New** (or "New Repository"). A "repository" is just a fancy word for a folder.
    3. Name your folder whatever you want (e.g., `client-pdf-maker`).
    4. Scroll down and click the green **Create repository** button.
    5. On the next screen, click the small link that says **"uploading an existing file"**.
    6. Drag and drop your `app.py` and `requirements.txt` files onto the screen.
    7. Click the green **Commit changes** button. (Commit just means "Save").
  
  
    1. Go to **share.streamlit.io** and click "Sign up". 
    2. Choose **Continue with GitHub**. This links your Kitchen to your Recipe Book.
    3. Once you are logged in, click the blue **Create app** button at the top right.
    4. Click **Yep, I have an app**.
    5. It will ask for your repository (the folder you just made on GitHub). Click the dropdown menu and select it.
    6. Ensure the "Main file path" says `app.py`.
    7. Click **Deploy!**
    
    > Streamlit will now take a few minutes to build your app. You will see some text scrolling by — just let it do its thing. When it's done, your website will appear on the screen!
  
  
    Look at the web address bar at the top of your screen (it will look something like `[https://your-app-name.streamlit.app](https://your-app-name.streamlit.app)`). 
    
    1. Open the web browser on your smartphone (Safari for iPhone, Chrome for Android).
    2. Type in that exact web address. Your AI generator will load on your phone!
    3. **To make it an app:** 
       * **iPhone:** Tap the "Share" button at the bottom (the square with the arrow pointing up) and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
       * **Android:** Tap the three dots at the top right of Chrome and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
       
    Now, you have an icon on your phone that opens your AI tool directly, just like a downloaded app.
